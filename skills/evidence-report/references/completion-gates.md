# Completion gates

A run is complete only when every applicable gate is true.

- Official primary sources were acquired or their unavailability was documented.
- Decisive claims include page, section, or question-level citations.
- Critical tables and conditions were visually inspected.
- Confirmed facts, interpretations, and unresolved matters are separated.
- Material numbers were cross-checked and their denominator or scope is clear.
- The requested final format was produced.
- Every DOCX page was rendered and visually inspected.
- The deliverable exists at the reported path and can be opened.
- Requested ingestion or archival work completed successfully.
- The final report lists unresolved blockers without disguising them as conclusions.

Use `scripts/check_completion.py run-state.json` for the deterministic final gate.
