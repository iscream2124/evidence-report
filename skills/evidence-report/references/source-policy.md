# Source and claim policy

## Authority order

1. Issuing agency, legislature, court, standards body, or original dataset
2. Official government mirror or archive
3. Institution directly responsible for implementation
4. Reputable secondary source
5. Aggregator, vendor summary, forum, or social post

Use lower levels to locate or corroborate evidence, not to replace an obtainable primary source.

## Claim states

- `confirmed`: directly supported by a primary source.
- `corroborated`: supported by multiple reliable sources but not the controlling primary document.
- `interpreted`: a reasoned application of confirmed facts.
- `unresolved`: the available sources do not decide the question.
- `conflicted`: authoritative sources disagree or apply to different dates or scopes.

For each decisive claim record the source title, issuing body, date, page or section, URL, and claim state.

## Common errors

- A category being eligible does not prove a particular applicant qualifies.
- No explicit prohibition does not guarantee selection or approval.
- Total budget is not a per-project maximum.
- A current-year notice does not establish next year's schedule.
- A generic guideline does not override a project-specific notice.
- A search snippet is not a substitute for the underlying document.
